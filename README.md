# Nelson Amplification Law (NAL)

**Author:** Nelson Chou (周端政)
**Status:** Public Concept
**Definition version:** NAL-DEF v1.0 (2026-07-24)
**Primary definition:** https://www.nelsonchou.com/nelson-amplification-law/

## Definition

Quality is the product of judgment density, machine amplification and review intensity.

## Formula

`Q = D × M × Φ`

- **Q** (Quality) = the quality of the final output
- **D** (Judgment Density) = how much verifiable judgment a human puts in at the input end
- **M** (Machine Amplification) = the rate at which AI amplifies that input
- **Φ** (Review Intensity) = the strength of human checking and veto at the output end

## Fingerprint sentence

> "What AI amplifies is not your ability. It is your own judgment density." — Nelson Chou, Nelson Amplification Law
